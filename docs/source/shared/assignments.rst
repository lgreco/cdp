Assignments & Exams
====================

Course performance is evaluated with a series of assignments. In a 200-level course and higher, homeworks and exams are week-long, take-home assignments. Depending on the course, there are between 8 and 12 assignments per term. Assignments are typically posted on Friday and due the following Friday. Solutions to the assignments are posted after the deadline.

There are no deadline expectations or make-up assignments in this course. See :ref:`Deadlines <deadlines>` and :ref:`Make-up policy <makeUp>` for details.
