Assignments & Exams
====================

Course performance is evaluated with a series of assignments. In a 200-level course and higher, homeworks are week-long, take-home assignments. Depending on the course, there are between 10 and 14 assignments per term. Assignments are typically posted on Friday and due the following Friday. Solutions to the assignments are posted after the deadline.

Exams are usually a mix of take-home work and in-person, oral one-one sessions with me. 

There are no deadline expectations or make-up assignments in this course. See :ref:`Deadlines <deadlines>` and :ref:`Make-up policy <makeUp>` for details.
