Reading material
----------------

A [currated reading list](https://learning.oreilly.com/playlists/a69fe214-ad19-4edf-aa81-e57c577ba365) is available on the O'Reilly platform. You may access it with your LUC email. Additional material will be distributed via the course's GitHub repository.

Two free Python books are also used as sources for some generated course material (see :doc:`textbook` for how they're used):

* `Think Python, 3rd edition <https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkPython/>`__, by Allen Downey — free online.

* `Introducing Python, 3rd edition <https://learning.oreilly.com/library/view/introducing-python-3rd/9781098174392/>`__, by Bill Lubanovic — on the O'Reilly platform above, free with your LUC email.
