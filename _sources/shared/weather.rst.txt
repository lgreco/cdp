Weather and Class Meetings Policy
=================================

In cases of severe or extreme weather, I will follow the university’s official operating status. I cannot independently cancel class or move meetings online unless the university formally declares a weather emergency or campus closure.

Unilateral changes—such as moving a single class online while the rest of campus remains in person—often create real problems for students. For example, if my class moves online but your next class is still on campus, that doesn't actually make your day easier. It just makes it more complicated.

Therefore, my policy is simple: if the university is open, our class will meet as scheduled.

That said, on days with challenging weather conditions, I will not take attendance. Your safety always comes first and if you decide to stay home, you should not worry about an absence.
