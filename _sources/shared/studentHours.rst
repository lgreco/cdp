Student hours (aka office hours)
================================


Student hours for the current semester are posted on `my schedule on Calendly <https://calendly.com/leo_irakliotis/20min>`_. 

You can schedule an appointment with me by finding the best time slot that works for you. If there are no available time slots or if they conflict with your schedule, please notify me, suggest a couple of other time slots that will work for you, and I will do my best to find us time to meet.

Walk-ins are welcome but `appointments are highly recommended <https://calendly.com/leo_irakliotis/15min>`_. Students with an appointment take precedence. My office is in **Doyle 207**. For online meetings, I use Zoom or Microsoft Teams.

*Why "student hours" and not "office hours"?* I prefer the term "student hours" because it emphasizes that these hours are dedicated to supporting students, rather than being solely about my availability in an office.
